Supreme Court Rules ISPs Not Contributorily Liable for User Piracy in Cox v. Sony

In a unanimous decision, the Court held that Cox Communications was not contributorily liable for copyright infringement simply because it knew some subscribers were pirating music. Requires affirmative intent or tailored service.
